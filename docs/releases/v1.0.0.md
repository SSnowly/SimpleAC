**SimpleAC 1.0.0**

**Install**
• Auto install: on first start SimpleAC adds `init.lua` to every resource's `fxmanifest.lua` for you
• First start only: joining is blocked with a big "RESTART THE SERVER" message until you restart, then never again
• MariaDB support (incl. 16.1)

**New protections**
• Unauthorized vehicle and explosion spawns are caught and deleted, with a whitelist for trusted resources
• Server-side speed check for on-foot and in-vehicle movement
• Player cleanup: removes everything a cheater spawned and revives their victims, runs automatically before a kick or ban

**Weapons**
• Damage and range limits now come from the real `weapons.meta` data for every weapon
• Custom weapons are picked up automatically from your resources, and you can override any of them
• Cancelled damage now resets the victim's health on the attacker's screen, so no more fake kills

**Defaults**
• Unauthorized vehicles and explosions, server speed hacks and NUI devtools are permanent bans
